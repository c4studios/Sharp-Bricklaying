Whether your project is in planning or ready to commence, we welcome early enquiries. With our schedule often committed up to three months in advance, this allows us to properly plan, coordinate, and deliver each project to our standard while aligning with your proposed timeframe.

Getting a useful quote back.

The more you can tell us up front, the faster we can come back with something worth reading. None of it is essential, so send what you have and we will work out the rest on the phone.

  • The suburb, and where the build is up to

    Slab down, frame up, or still at the drawing stage. It tells us where the job sits against our schedule.

  • Plans or engineering drawings, if you have them

    Email them straight through to luke@sharpbricklaying.com.au. Anything readable helps, including a photo of a printed set.

  • The kind of brickwork involved

    Face brick, breeze blocks, rendered, undecided? We can help you run through some options depending on your thoughts on design.

  • Roughly when you need a crew on site

    Even a rough month helps. Our schedule is often committed up to three months in advance.
