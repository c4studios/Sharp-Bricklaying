15+ Yrs Experience
The Craftsman

Built by Hand.
Every Time.

Sharp Bricklaying was founded in 2016, starting with a single project home slab and a clear focus - deliver brickwork that's clean, consistent, and built to a high standard from day one.

Growth came through consistency, not volume. Early projects turned into ongoing relationships, and over time the work progressed into larger, more boutique residential builds as he continued to reach out and network with higher-end builders - often in the same areas Luke grew up, which made the journey that bit more meaningful.

With over 15 years in the trade, Luke has seen both sides of the industry - from rushed, rough work to detailed architectural builds where collaboration with clients and builders brings a vision together. Through both busy periods and quieter cycles, Sharp Bricklayings standard has remained the same.

Today, Sharp Bricklaying works on a range of residential and architectural projects, with a focus on delivering brickwork that complements the design and reflects the level of thought behind each build.

Equally important is the team behind it. Luke takes pride in developing his crew, passing on a clear standard and giving apprentices the opportunity to learn and carry forward a level of craftsmanship he believes is becoming less common.

Sharp Bricklaying isn't built to be the biggest crew in Perth. The focus is on taking on the right projects, building strong relationships, and leaving behind work the team is genuinely proud of and a gallery to show off to you.

"In an ever changing industry, our standards will always remain the same."
