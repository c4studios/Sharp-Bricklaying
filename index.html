Luke's Story

Our Name, Our Story.
Our Standard.

Sharp Bricklaying was founded in 2016 with a simple focus: deliver brickwork that is clean, consistent, and held to a higher standard. What began with a single project home slab has grown through reputation, strong relationships, and a commitment to quality over volume.

Over the years, that approach has led Sharp Bricklaying into larger boutique residential and architectural projects, working alongside builders and clients who value detail, communication, and craftsmanship.

With over 15 years in the trade, Luke has worked across all corners of the industry—from fast-paced volume construction to intricate architectural builds—shaping a clear understanding of what quality workmanship should look like, and what it often lacks.

Today, Sharp Bricklaying focuses on residential and architectural masonry projects that complement the design intent of each build. We prioritise building long term relationships, taking as much pride in our brickwork as we do our network. This is yet another aspect that is getting lost in this industry.

Equally important is the team behind the business. A strong emphasis is placed on maintaining standards on site, developing the next generation of tradesmen, and preserving a level of craftsmanship that is becoming increasingly uncommon.

Sharp Bricklaying was never built to be the biggest crew in Perth. The focus has always been on taking on the right projects, building lasting relationships, and delivering work the team is genuinely proud to stand behind.

“I’ve always believed in delivering the extra 1% - not just in our brickwork, but across every part of the process. The goal is simple: a detailed finished product for the client, the unseen extras that set up following trades properly, and an experience that everyone involved - enjoys being part of. Leaving behind work we’re proud of and impressions of myself and my team that are remembered.” — Luke Sharp

Sponsor Profile

Mudboards Australia

Mudboards Australia is bringing a smarter alternative to the standard ply and metal mudboards seen across site. Innovation in bricklaying doesn't come around often, which is exactly why products like this stand out - a genuine rethink of something we use every single day.

Manufactured from UV-resistant HDPE plastic, these 600x600x10mm boards are built to handle harsh Australian conditions without the drawbacks of traditional materials. At just 3.4kg, they're lightweight enough for easy handling around site with the built-in handles, while still feeling solid under daily use. This is one of those purchases where you buy it once and you're set for a very long time - not constantly replacing bent metal boards or carting around worn-out alternatives.

Compared to timber boards that absorb moisture or metal boards that become messy and harder to maintain, the mud works beautifully on these, creating a smoother, more practical working surface without drawing moisture from the mud, helping it stay workable for longer before going off.

The easy-clean surface means wash-up is effortless at the end of the day, and no more banging those metal boards with your trowels every morning.

Safe to say, we never knew mudboards could be this exciting. This has definitely become a permanent replacement in our team.
