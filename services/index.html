Architectural Brickwork Perth

Bespoke architectural brickwork, renovations and extensions, and complete bricklaying services for builders, architects, developers and private clients across Perth.

Bespoke brickwork. A different approach.

At Sharp Bricklaying, our work goes beyond conventional brickwork. We specialise in bespoke architectural projects across Perth, working closely with boutique builders, developers and architects to bring considered designs to life. We also accommodate owner-builders and a range of residential projects, from complete custom homes and larger residential developments to smaller bespoke builds.

Our approach is anything but standard. From curved walls and intricate hit-and-miss brickwork to decorative features, archways, detailed bonding patterns and one-off architectural designs, we understand how individual elements come together to achieve the overall design intent. It’s often the smaller details, the ones easily overlooked, that make the greatest difference to the finished project.

We work with premium brick products, including Krause Emperor bricks, exploring different sizes, textures, finishes and bonding patterns to create brickwork that complements the surrounding architecture. Alternative treatments, including acid sponge finishes where suitable, can introduce variations in texture and appearance, while carefully selected mortar colours and jointing techniques can completely change how the same brick presents.

From flush and raked joints to more specialised joint profiles, oxide-coloured mortar and custom finishing techniques, we consider how each detail affects the overall appearance of the brickwork. Achieving the right result isn’t simply about choosing a brick. It’s about understanding how the brick, mortar, joint profile and finish work together, while maintaining consistency throughout the entire project.

Architectural brickwork requires a different approach

No two architectural projects are the same, and we don’t believe in approaching them as though they are.

We enjoy being involved in the design process, working alongside architects, builders and clients to explore the practical application of their ideas. We’re always happy to offer recommendations on materials, construction methods and finishing techniques, particularly where a design calls for something outside conventional bricklaying.

Our focus is on understanding the original design intent and finding the best way to achieve it, without overlooking the practical considerations behind the finished appearance.

Residential renovations & extensions

Residential renovations and extensions require a different level of understanding, particularly when integrating new brickwork into an existing structure.

Unlike a new build, there are often details that can’t be fully accounted for in the drawings. Existing walls, variations in construction methods, structural conditions and even minor inconsistencies can influence how the new work needs to be approached.

We understand the importance of achieving a seamless transition between old and new, both cosmetically and structurally. Whether matching existing brickwork, working with older construction methods or incorporating contemporary architectural features into an established home, the finer details require careful consideration.

Renovations and extensions also bring unforeseen challenges that no amount of planning can completely eliminate. Being able to recognise issues, adapt to site conditions and work through practical solutions is an important part of the process. Our experience allows us to approach these situations with an understanding of what’s required, working alongside builders and other relevant trades to achieve a result that respects the existing structure and the client’s vision.

Complete bricklaying services

We offer flexible bricklaying services for boutique residential and architectural projects throughout Perth, from labour-only arrangements through to complete brickwork packages. We accommodate a wide range of clients and project sizes, including boutique builders, developers, owner-builders and private clients.

Our services can include experienced bricklayers, material supply, specialist equipment and site clean-up.

Builders and clients are welcome to supply their own materials or have us arrange everything as part of the project. Where materials are purchased independently, we’re happy to provide guidance on suitable brick products and materials.

Whether we’re completing the brickwork for an entire custom home, a larger residential development, extending an existing residence or working through a single architectural feature, our focus remains the same: quality workmanship, consistent finishes and attention to the details that bring the design together.
