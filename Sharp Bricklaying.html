The Craftsman

Built by Hand.
Every Time.

Luke Sharp started Sharp Bricklaying with a simple belief: if your name's going on a wall, that wall should be something to be proud of. Based in Perth, WA, Luke and his team bring the same care to a modest extension as they do to a multi-million dollar prestige build.

That reputation has earned Sharp Bricklaying a place alongside Perth's most respected builders — Zorzi, Beamond, Giorgi, and Summit Homes. These aren't builders who take risks on their trades. They come back because the work is consistently right.

"We work with some of the biggest builders in Perth — and they keep calling back."

Sharp Bricklaying has also been featured on national television alongside Summit Homes, bringing a bit of Perth's finest brickwork to screens across the country.
