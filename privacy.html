Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Who We Are

Sharp Bricklaying ("we", "us", "our") is a residential bricklaying business based in Perth, Western Australia. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through our website at sharpbricklaying.net.

Information We Collect

When you use our contact form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Project type and message details

We do not collect financial information, passwords, or sensitive personal data through this website.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Provide a quote for your project
  • Communicate with you about your bricklaying needs

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

Our contact form is processed through Formspree, a third-party form handling service. Your submitted data is transmitted securely to Formspree's servers and then forwarded to our email. Please refer to Formspree's Privacy Policy for details on their data handling.

We use Google Fonts to display typefaces on our website. This involves your browser making requests to Google's servers. Please see Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies for tracking or advertising. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy accordingly and implement appropriate consent mechanisms.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information we hold from misuse, interference, and loss, as well as unauthorised access, modification, or disclosure. Our website uses HTTPS encryption for all data transmission.

Your Rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act 1988, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at luke@sharpbricklaying.net.
