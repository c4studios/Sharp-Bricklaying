Join the Sharp crew.

We’re looking to fill a few roles here at Sharp Bricklaying, and we’re always keeping an eye out for the right people to join our team.

Whether you’re being given no room or opportunity to grow in your current team, want consistent work with full weeks and weekly pays, or want a change in dynamic, let’s see if you fit what we’re looking for.

We’re a small team, and that’s how we intend to stay. Our focus has never been on building the biggest crew, but on delivering the same consistent standard of work across every project, for every builder and client we work with.

We’d rather maintain a smaller team of skilled tradespeople who take pride in their work than grow into a large crew focused purely on getting bricks in the wall.

We’re not about the shouting, rushing around or constantly pushing to get more bricks in. We work on complex architectural projects that require patience, attention to detail and an understanding of what you’re building.

We take our work seriously, but that doesn’t mean the site has to be a stressful place to work. We value people who can work independently, take initiative and hold themselves accountable for the quality of their work.

If you’re someone who takes pride in your trade and appreciates quality workmanship, we’d like to hear from you.

We have consistent work booked through until mid-2027, with a strong pipeline of bespoke residential projects across Perth.

Open roles

  • Bricklayer

    Experienced

    We’re looking for one more experienced bricklayer to join our team.

    Someone who takes pride in their work and is looking for consistent, ongoing projects. We’re already booked through until mid-2027, with a strong pipeline of architectural and bespoke residential builds.

    Ideally, we’re after someone with experience in architectural brickwork, but at a minimum, attention to detail is non-negotiable.

    You need to be confident working independently, understand your scope of work and take responsibility for the quality of what you produce.

    We’re not looking for someone who needs constant direction or reminding of the basics. We want another bricklayer who knows their trade, takes initiative and holds themselves to the same standards we do.

    • Strong residential experience in Perth
    • A high standard of workmanship and attention to detail
    Apply for this role

  • Leading hand / supervisor

    Experienced

    We’re not looking for someone to manage a huge crew or spend their day chasing people around site.

    We’re after someone who can confidently read drawings, set out and organise work, guide our younger apprentices and work alongside myself and the other bricklayers to keep things running smoothly.

    Someone who can think ahead, stay on top of what needs doing and take ownership of their work without needing constant direction.

    You’ll still be on the tools. We’re simply looking for someone who can take on a little more responsibility and help maintain the standard of work we expect across our projects.

    Not quite at that level yet? If you’re an experienced bricklayer looking to step into a leading hand role, we’re open to that too. Send us a message and we can have a chat about your current experience, capabilities and where you’d like to progress.
