Sponsor Profile

Mudboards Australia

Mudboards Australia is bringing a smarter alternative to the standard ply and metal mudboards seen across site. Innovation in bricklaying doesn't come around often, which is exactly why products like this stand out - a genuine rethink of something we use every single day.

Manufactured from UV-resistant HDPE plastic, these 600x600x10mm boards are built to handle harsh Australian conditions without the drawbacks of traditional materials. At just 3.4kg, they're lightweight enough for easy handling around site with the built-in handles, while still feeling solid under daily use. This is one of those purchases where you buy it once and you're set for a very long time - not constantly replacing bent metal boards or carting around worn-out alternatives.

Compared to timber boards that absorb moisture or metal boards that become messy and harder to maintain, the mud works beautifully on these, creating a smoother, more practical working surface without drawing moisture from the mud, helping it stay workable for longer before going off.

The easy-clean surface means wash-up is effortless at the end of the day, and no more banging those metal boards with your trowels every morning.

Safe to say, we never knew mudboards could be this exciting. This has definitely become a permanent replacement in our team.

BrickieGrip

BrickieGrip is a durable over-grip designed to wrap around your bricklayer's trowel handle to improve grip, comfort, and control while working.

Developed and tested by an Australian bricklayer with over 20 years on the tools, BrickieGrip helps reduce slipping caused by sweat, water, and mortar during long days on site.

  • Improves grip and control
  • Water-resistant material
  • Designed for tough job-site conditions
  • Lightweight and easy to install
